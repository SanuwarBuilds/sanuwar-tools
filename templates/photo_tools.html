PHOTO GUIDE

Resize and compress photos for forms and printing

SanuwarTools runs entirely in your browser, so your photo is never uploaded. Use it for passport and visa photos, PAN and voter ID forms, exam applications, profile pictures and everyday sharing.

Exact pixel sizes

Type the width and height your form asks for, or lock a 1:1, 3:4 or 4:3 ratio. The preview always shows the final crop.

KB-limited uploads

Set Maximum size in KB and export JPG. If the first encode is too large, the tool lowers quality only as much as needed to fit.

Passport and print sheets

35 × 45 mm presets cover common passport and visa sizes. Build a 4 × 6 inch or A4 sheet, then print at actual size.
