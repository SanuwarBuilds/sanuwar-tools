Privacy policy

An overview of the information used when you visit SanuwarTools.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Who operates this site

SanuwarTools is maintained by Sanuwar. For privacy questions, deletion requests or concerns about content, use the channels on our contact page. Please do not post passwords, payment details or identity documents in public messages.

Site operation and storage

Our hosting provider processes requests, including IP addresses, browser information and server logs, to deliver and protect the website. Session cookies support admin authentication, request security and poll voting. Your browser may store your theme preference and purchase receipts locally. Clearing browser storage can remove access to receipts; retain your order reference before doing so.

Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your account identifier, verified email address and name so we can recognise your visitor session. We do not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Drive or contacts. The sign-in session is stored in a protected browser cookie and expires after two hours. You can sign out from your account page.

Downloads and payments

Files may be delivered by external services such as GitHub or Cloudinary, which receive your request when you open a download or preview. Where paid downloads are available, order details and payment status are processed by the site's configured payment service to verify access. Never send your UPI PIN or banking password to us. Admin catalogue content is stored using our connected repository and storage providers.

Google advertising

We use Google AdSense to display ads on public tool and download pages, subject to Google's approval and availability. Google and its partners may use cookies, local storage, IP addresses and device or browser information to deliver, personalize and measure advertising, limit frequency and prevent fraud. Third-party advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this and other websites.

You can manage personalized advertising at Google My Ad Center and learn how Google uses information from partner sites. You can also manage cookies through your browser. Where consent is required, the consent message provides your advertising choices. This privacy page does not load advertising tags.

External tools and links

Linked tools, social profiles, payment services and download hosts have their own privacy policies. Information you submit to those services is handled by them. Review their policies before uploading personal files or making a payment.

Retention and your choices

Information is kept as needed to operate the service, maintain purchase access, investigate abuse and meet applicable obligations. Provider retention periods vary. Contact us with the relevant order reference or page URL for access, correction or deletion requests. We may need to verify your request before changing records. Do not include financial secrets in your request.

Updates

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above shows the latest revision.
