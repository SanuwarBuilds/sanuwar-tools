04 — About

A small toolkit for everyday work

SanuwarTools is a free, browser-based toolkit maintained by Sanuwar. The photo resizer and compressor crops images to exact pixels, DPI and KB limits for passport, PAN, voter ID and exam forms — entirely on your device.

Explore the downloads library for Android apps, files and albums, or open the other web tools in the collection. New modules are added as they are ready; no account is needed for public tools.
