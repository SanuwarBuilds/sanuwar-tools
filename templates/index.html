04 — About

A small toolkit for everyday work

SanuwarTools is a free, browser-based toolkit maintained by Sanuwar. The collection now includes {{ catalog.tools | length }} browser tools for text, encoding, hashes, images, PDFs, networking, security and everyday maths, plus the photo resizer and compressor that crops images to exact pixels, DPI and KB limits for passport, PAN, voter ID and exam forms — entirely on your device.

Explore the browser tools or the full tool page, browse the downloads library for Android apps, files and albums, and open the source directories for movies, anime, music and more. New modules are added as they are ready; no account is needed for public tools.
