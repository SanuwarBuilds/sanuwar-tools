# SanuwarTools

## Purpose

SanuwarTools brings useful browser tools, downloadable files, directories and education resources into one public web workspace. Visitors can find a task, open its tool or resource, and return to recently used or saved tools.

## Platform

Web. The public experience supports desktop and mobile browsers. Administration is a separate authenticated layout.

## Users and jobs

Repository-grounded working context: visitors need practical tasks such as resizing a photo, merging PDFs, formatting JSON, checking a form upload or calculating an amount. They can also browse published downloads and Eduhelp study resources. More specific audience, business and positioning claims remain undecided.

## Capabilities and workflows

- Shared global search finds public tools, files, downloads and other indexed resources.
- Tools remain reachable through the complete catalogue and retained guide links.
- Favorites and up to six recent tool entries are stored in the visitor's local browser; these are not an account-sync promise. Storage failures must not be represented as successful saves.
- Public downloads come from published catalogue data; availability and empty states reflect real configuration.
- Eduhelp provides study resources and interactive science labs.
- Sign-in/account access and the existing administrative workflow remain available.

## Constraints

Keep public resource links safe and retain the distinction between public and private content. Preserve existing task functionality and navigation while changing presentation. Do not invent catalogue entries, usage statistics, testimonials or deployment evidence. Respect keyboard access, labeled controls, reduced motion and saved theme preferences.

## Brand and voice

The product name is SanuwarTools. Public interface copy is concise English with task-based labels. The user-approved visual reference is recorded in DESIGN.md and the homepage surface brief; visual tokens and page composition do not belong in this product document.

## Evidence and unresolved decisions

This document captures implemented repository capabilities and the approved implementation scope. Audience prioritization, commercial positioning and success metrics were not established in this task and remain open. It makes no claim that deployment or runtime verification has completed.
