Hydrophilic Ionic Head (—COO⁻Na⁺)

Function / Role

Water-loving (polar) carboxylate group that dissolves in water and keeps the micelle suspended.

What It Connects To

Covalently bonded to the C₁₇H₃₅ hydrocarbon tail; electrostatically attracts polar H₂O molecules & Na⁺ counter-ions.

What Happens When Operating

Faces outward toward water, creating a negatively charged spherical shield that repels other micelles (preventing dirt from settling back).
